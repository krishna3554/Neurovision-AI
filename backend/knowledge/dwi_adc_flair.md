# Diffusion MRI in stroke

Diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI) is sensitive to acute infarction. Restricted
diffusion appears bright on DWI and dark on the apparent diffusion coefficient
(ADC) map. DWI hyperintensity with ADC hypointensity indicates acute cytotoxic
edema from ischemia.

FLAIR hyperintensity develops over hours. A DWI-FLAIR mismatch (bright DWI,
normal FLAIR) suggests onset within roughly 4.5 hours, but this is probabilistic
and must be interpreted by a clinician with the full clinical picture.
